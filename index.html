01 — About

Building with curiosity.
Learning with purpose.

I'm a developer who enjoys the entire arc of making something — from the first rough idea, through the parts that don't work yet, to the moment it finally clicks into place. I like the process of coding as much as the result: reading through a new library, rebuilding something I've made before in a cleaner way, or picking apart a bug until it makes sense. Most of what I build starts as a small experiment I was curious about, and I try to keep it that way — practical, a little exploratory, and always a chance to learn something I didn't know going in.
